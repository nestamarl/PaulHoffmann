## 🗺️ Our Route

**August 11–23, 2026** – Toronto and Niagara Falls family trip with Leon and Emil, getting around with a rental car for the whole trip.

---

## Day 1 – Monday, August 11

Arrival in Toronto. Check in at the **Hilton Garden Inn Toronto Airport West/Mississauga** – easy first day, explore the neighbourhood, grab dinner downtown.

## Day 2 – Tuesday, August 12

**Toronto Zoo & African Safari**

One of the largest zoos in the world – a full day for sure. Don't miss the African Savanna section. Bring snacks and comfortable shoes, the zoo is huge!

🎟️ Tip: Book tickets online in advance, or include it in the [Toronto CityPASS](https://www.citypass.com/toronto) to save money.

## Day 3 – Wednesday, August 13

**CN Tower & Ripley's Aquarium**

Start the morning at the **CN Tower** – the glass floor is a must, the kids will love (or hate) it! Right next door is **Ripley's Aquarium of Canada**, perfect for the afternoon. Both are included in the Toronto CityPASS.

## Day 4 – Thursday, August 14

**Toronto Island**

Take the ferry from Jack Layton Ferry Terminal to the Toronto Islands. Beaches, bike rentals, and a great view of the skyline. The water is calm and perfect for kids.

## Day 5–6 – Friday–Saturday, August 15–16

**Africa Lion Safari & overnight in Hamilton**

About 1.5 hours from Toronto in Hamilton. Drive through the safari park with animals roaming freely around the car – always a highlight for the kids! Check out of the Toronto hotel in the morning and spend the night in Hamilton at the **Visitors Inn** before heading on to Niagara Falls.

## Day 6–7 – Saturday–Sunday, August 16–17

**Niagara Falls (1 overnight stay)**

Stay at the **Hilton Niagara Falls/Fallsview Hotel & Suites** – absolutely worth it, especially at night when the falls are lit up!

Things to do:

- Walk along the promenade
- Hornblower/Niagara City Cruises boat tour
- Journey Behind the Falls

## Day 8–12 – Tuesday–Saturday, August 18–22

**Cottage days at the beach**

Staying at the **Kosecki Kottage** in Port Burwell, a cozy 2-bedroom cottage close to Lake Erie's beaches – less than a 10-minute walk to the public beach, or a short drive to Port Burwell Provincial Park with over 2.5 km of sand. Relaxing days: swimming, exploring the little town, maybe a bonfire in the backyard.

## Day 13 – Sunday, August 23

Departure day. Flight back at 2:00 PM – head home with lots of great memories (and probably tired kids 😄).
