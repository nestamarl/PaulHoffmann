### Day 1 – Friday, July 17

- Departure from Fredericton ~12:00
- Lunch in St. John
- Ferry 14:15 → ~17:00 arrival in Digby
{{< /tripday >}}
<!-- FM:Snippet:End -->

From Fredericton we drive to St. John (~1:15 h) and then cross over on the [MV Fundy Rose](https://www.ferries.ca/nb-ns-ferry) to Digby in Nova Scotia. Departure is at 170 Digby Ferry Road, Saint John, NB, E2M 0B2 at 14:15 – you need to be at check-in at least one hour beforehand. The crossing takes about 2 to 2.5 hours.

Ferry cost: CAD 122 (2 adults + car, children under 5 travel free)

**Accommodation (reserved):** 

[Coastal Inn Digby](https://coastalinns.com/digby/) – CAD 160 + tax, including breakfast (via booking.com)

### Day 2 – Saturday, July 18

- Digby → Bridgewater (~2–2.5 h)

{{< /tripday >}}
<!-- FM:Snippet:End -->

From Digby it's about a 2-hour drive towards Lunenburg (UNESCO World Heritage Site), with Kejimkujik National Park roughly halfway along the route. In Lunenburg, the [Fisheries Museum of the Atlantic](https://fisheriesmuseum.novascotia.ca/) is well worth a visit. If we're still up for it, we can also stop in Mahone Bay – depending on how we feel.

**Excursions:**
- Mahone Bay
- Lunenburg

**Accommodation (reserved):** 

[Bridgewater Hotel](http://www.bridgewaterhotel.ca/) – CAD 168 + tax, breakfast optionally bookable for CAD 10

### Day 3/4 – Sunday/Monday, July 19–20

- Bridgewater → Mahone Bay → Peggy's Cove → Halifax (~1 h)

{{< /tripday >}}
<!-- FM:Snippet:End -->

Peggy's Cove is almost on the way to Halifax and easy to fit in depending on the weather – best visited in the morning. Along the route there are also several beaches, e.g. Queensland Beach, as well as an ice cream stand – though you do need to briefly exit the highway.

**Excursions in Halifax:**
- [Waterfront Boardwalk](https://novascotia.com/get-inspired/top-25/halifax-waterfront/)
- [Museum of Natural History](https://naturalhistory.novascotia.ca/)
- [Discovery Centre](https://thediscoverycentre.ca/)

**Accommodation (reserved):** 

[Future Inn Halifax](https://www.futureinns.com/)

### Day 5/6/7 – Tuesday, July 21–24

Halifax → PEI (~3 h)

{{< /tripday >}}
<!-- FM:Snippet:End -->

From Halifax to Caribou for the ferry is about 2 hours. The [ferry](https://www.ferries.ca/ns-pei-ferry) from Caribou to Wood Islands on PEI costs CAD 11.50 per adult – children travel free. Daily departures at 07:00, 08:30, 10:00, 11:45, 13:30, 15:15, 17:00 and 18:30, check-in at least 40 minutes before departure. Journey time is 75 minutes.

On the way to the ferry you could stop at the [Aviation Museum](https://acamuseum.ca/).

**Accommodation:** 

[Cavendish Motel (reserved)](https://www.cavendishmotel.com/)
